Welcome! I am a Research Fellow in the the Department of Data Science, [Dana-Farber Cancer Institute](https://www.dana-farber.org/) and affiliated with [Department of Biostatistics at the Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health](https://hsph.harvard.edu) and in Boston, MA. My current research, involves developing Bayesian Discrete Matrix Factorization algorithms to study chromosomal aberrations inherent in multiple myeloma, with the goal of uncovering meaningful biological insights through Boolean operations in the semi-ring framework. I hold a Ph.D. in Probability and Statistics from [CIMAT](https://www.cimat.mx/) and have experience spanning statistical modeling, data science, and computational biology. My previous experience include: a senior lecturer at Bomet University College in Kenya, where my research focused on financial time series, exchange rate volatility, and macroeconomic forecasting, alongside methodological advances in multivariate statistics and classification algorithms for biological data among others.
